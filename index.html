About

A little about me.

Coach. Dad. Arkansan. Yes, I fought in the UFC — but on the mat, what matters is what we work on today.

I'm TJ Brown. Born in Pine Bluff, raised in Arkansas, and I've called Little Rock home for over a decade. My day-to-day is pretty simple: teach in the mornings and afternoons, hang out with my son Kyler, and try to leave the gym a little better than I found it.

I came up at Westside MMA in Little Rock under Coach Roli Delgado, who later promoted me to BJJ black belt. I had a twelve-year career as a pro fighter — competed in the UFC from 2020 to 2024 — but honestly the best part of the journey has been the people I've gotten to work with along the way. Teaching is where I'm at my happiest.

If you're new to martial arts, welcome — I love working with beginners. If you're an active competitor and need a sharp pair of eyes, let's talk about your camp. If you're a parent looking for something positive for your kid, my kids' lessons are some of my favorite hours of the week.

The Story

Why I coach.

There's a longer story underneath all this — and it's why teaching is more important to me than fighting ever was.

I was eighteen, off-track at the start of college football, and about to be a father for the first time. I walked into a gym in Little Rock not really knowing what I was doing. A couple months later I won my first amateur fight in ten seconds. I was hooked.

I grew up without my dad around. I didn't meet him for the first time until I was 32 years old. So when my son Kyler showed up, I made one promise — that he'd never wonder where I was. That promise has shaped everything else: how I train, how I coach, why I stayed sober, why I do this for a living.

"It's a beautiful thing to be able to break that chain. My son is never gonna ask those questions." — TJ, on UFC.com

Coaching is how I pay it forward. Every kid that walks into one of my classes, every adult that finally gets brave enough to try this stuff — that's where the work actually happens. The fight career was great. This part is better.

FAQ

Good questions.

Stuff people ask me all the time. If yours isn't here, just shoot me a message.

I've never trained before. Is that okay?
More than okay — it's my favorite kind of student. The vast majority of people I teach are total beginners. Wear something comfortable, bring water, and show up. I'll handle the rest.
My kid wants to try — what's the right age?
I work with kids ages 6 to 14. Younger ones are about discipline, listening, and having fun. Older kids get more technique and structure. Parents are welcome to watch every session — I want you to feel comfortable.
What should I wear?
Athletic clothes, no shoes on the mat. If we're doing BJJ, I can lend you a gi. Bring water and a towel. That's it.
Where are the sessions held?
Little Rock, Arkansas. I'll send you the exact address when we lock in your time. For seminars or out-of-state work, I travel — just ask.
How much does it cost?
Depends on the format (private, kids, small group, fight camp) and how often you want to train. Send me a booking request with your goals and I'll come back with options that make sense for you.
Are you still fighting?
Off and on. Most of my time goes to coaching now. If I'm in camp I'll let you know — but the lessons keep rolling.
Do you do seminars?
Yes — at your gym, my gym, anywhere. Reach out through the booking form and tell me what your group wants to focus on. I'll travel for the right fit.
