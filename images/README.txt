DROP TJ'S PHOTOS HERE
=====================

The website is wired to look for two specific filenames. Drop them
in this folder and they'll appear automatically — no code changes
needed.

REQUIRED FILES:

  tj-hero.jpg         (action / fight / stance photo — shows in hero)
  tj-coaching.jpg     (TJ teaching / holding pads — shows in About)

SPECS:

  Format:    .jpg or .png (just keep the filename the same)
  Hero:      portrait orientation, 4:5 aspect ratio works best
             ideal size: 1200 x 1500 px or larger
  Coaching:  landscape orientation, 4:3 aspect ratio works best
             ideal size: 1200 x 900 px or larger

NOTES:

- Photos crop from the top so the subject's face stays visible.
- Keep file size under ~500KB each for fast loading (Tinify.com is great).
- DO NOT use Google Images / UFC press photos — those are owned by
  Zuffa LLC or Getty and we'd get hit with a copyright takedown.
  Best source: TJ's own phone / Instagram / a local photographer.

That's it. Drop the files in, refresh the site, done.
